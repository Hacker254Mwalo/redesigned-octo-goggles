# Poultry & Livestock Listing Policy

## Purpose

MtaaMarket may host **owner-approved local listings** for lawful poultry and livestock, starting with chickens and other ordinary farm animals that can be safely and transparently handled in Siaya County. This is a physical-product and live-animal discovery category, not a livestock auction, veterinary service, transport service, slaughter service, or payment/escrow product.

## Launch boundary

| Allowed after owner approval | Not allowed on MtaaMarket |
|---|---|
| Chickens, ducks, turkeys, rabbits, goats, sheep, cattle, pigs, and other ordinary domestic farm animals, subject to owner review. | Wild, protected, captive wild, endangered, illegal, stolen, injured, visibly sick, or distressed animals. |
| Original photographs, truthful description, animal type/breed where known, age or age range, quantity, sex where relevant, location area, asking price, and collection preference. | Copied supplier photos/text, health or breeding guarantees without evidence, automated bids, anonymous auctions, or misleading availability claims. |
| Buyer contact remains platform-mediated until the owner confirms the lawful local fulfilment route. | Automatic checkout, courier promises, payment collection, transport booking, cross-county movement arrangements, or buyer/seller contact disclosure by default. |

## Required owner review

Every live-animal listing will begin as a draft and needs manual owner approval before it is public. The owner must confirm that the seller is an approved vendor, the material is original, the listing describes a domestic farm animal, the location is in the intended service area, and the seller accepts the welfare and lawful-sale declaration.

The listing interface will require sellers to state that the animal is not visibly sick or in distress, is not offered for fighting or cruelty, and that any movement, veterinary, disease-control, identification, or local authority requirement will be checked before collection or transport. Kenya’s animal-welfare framework includes anti-cruelty obligations and transport requirements; local animal movement permits are also an official government procedure. [1] [2]

> **MtaaMarket does not certify animal health, ownership, breed, fertility, pregnancy, vaccination, disease status, age, or legal movement eligibility.** Buyers and sellers must confirm these facts directly through the owner-managed fulfilment process and the appropriate local veterinary or county authority where needed.

## Information shown publicly

Public discovery will show the listing title, permitted animal type, quantity, price, broad Siaya area, seller-approved status, original photos, and a clear “confirmation required” status. Exact farm addresses, buyer contact details, veterinary documents, payment details, and transport instructions remain private until the owner has reviewed the request.

## Fulfilment and welfare safeguard

MtaaMarket will keep livestock fulfilment manual. A buyer may submit an interest/request; the owner then confirms availability, pickup location, safe handling, any applicable movement documentation, and the final payment instruction. No live animal should be transported or collected through the platform until those checks are complete. The platform will remove or suspend a listing or vendor when safety, welfare, legality, ownership, truthfulness, or platform-mediation requirements are not met.
