

## Production vendor lifecycle

MtaaMarket uses a one-time account gate for third-party vendors. A user verifies their email, accepts the vendor agreement, and submits a vendor application. The owner reviews the vendor account and may approve or suspend it. Until approval, Seller Studio remains unavailable and no product record is created.

After approval, the same vendor account can publish physical-product listings normally. The listing path validates required fields, image type and size, category rules, stock quantity, and livestock safeguards before creating an `ACTIVE` product record. Products do not wait in a second manual approval queue by default. The owner can still hide, flag, reject, or delete a listing later through moderation, and policy or safety rules remain enforced at submission time.

The founder account is an owner account rather than an ordinary vendor application. Founder owner access therefore unlocks Seller Studio directly and does not appear in the vendor-approval queue. Founder and approved-vendor listings are still visible to owner moderation controls after publication.

This model follows the practical distinction visible in established marketplaces: seller onboarding and account access are handled separately from seller product operations, while post-submission content rules, reporting, and moderation remain available.
